# Research Discipline

`research/` contains bounded investigation of existing external or project evidence. Research gathers and synthesizes evidence; it does not create new experimental evidence.

1. Each research question MUST use one `RNNN-<slug>/` directory. IDs are stable and MUST NOT be reused.

2. `question.md` MUST define the bounded question, scope, and what decision or uncertainty the research is intended to inform.

3. Search only as broadly as necessary to resolve the material alternatives. Do not collect information merely for completeness.

4. Prefer primary, authoritative, and current sources where available. Preserve material dates, versions, populations, conditions, and source limitations.

5. Distinguish:

   * `SOURCE FACT`;
   * `INFERENCE`;
   * `SYNTHESIS`;
   * `UNRESOLVED`.

6. Do not treat repeated reporting of the same underlying source as independent evidence.

7. Seek material counterevidence before settling on a consequential conclusion.

8. Do not force consensus where credible evidence remains conflicted. Preserve disagreement and state what would resolve it.

9. `sources.md` records only sources materially used in the research. Do not build exhaustive bibliographies without a concrete need.

10. `findings.md` MUST state the answer to the research question, decisive supporting evidence, material counterevidence, uncertainty, and applicability limits.

11. Stop when further research is unlikely to materially change the answer. Do not continue searching merely to increase confidence.

12. Research findings are evidence, not project authority. They do not by themselves approve architecture, implementation, parameter selection, or production change.

13. If answering the question requires generating new measurements, simulations, benchmarks, or runtime observations, create an experiment instead of silently turning research into experimentation.

14. Keep research artifacts concise. Reference sources rather than copying large source material into the repository.
